# Wolfmark branding

Wolfmark's source code is licensed under GPL-3.0-only. That license permits copying, modification, and redistribution under its terms; this branding policy does not limit those software freedoms.

The Wolfmark name, logo, wordmark, and official release presentation identify the upstream Wolfmark project. Modified or independently distributed builds should use names and presentation that do not imply they are official Wolfmark releases, endorsed by Wolfmark, or published by ItsW0lfiy.

Accurate factual references to Wolfmark, attribution, compatibility statements, and descriptions of a fork's origin remain welcome. This policy is intentionally separate from the software license.
